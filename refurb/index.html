How we grade

Grades describe how a laptop looks. Every grade works the same: all 42 checks passed, battery at least 80% of its original capacity, 12-month warranty.

  1. A

    Looks nearly new

    No marks you’d notice from arm’s length. The screen is flawless.

  2. B

    Light signs of use

    A few fine scratches on the lid or base. The screen has no marks.

  3. C

    Visible wear, lowest price

    Scuffs or small dents on the body. Works exactly like an A.

What happens to every laptop before it reaches you

  1. Checked inSerial number logged, ownership and theft status verified.

  4. Parts replacedNew battery if it’s below 80%. Worn keys and hinges swapped.
