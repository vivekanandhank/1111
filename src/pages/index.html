FAQs

Common questions

How long does it take to deploy your solution?
Deployment timelines depend on solution complexity. VR/CBT modules can be deployed in 2–4 weeks. Smart Labelling or AiVA may take 4–8 weeks depending on asset volume and integrations.
Is this a one-time solution or a subscription-based service?
We offer both CAPEX and OPEX models. Most clients choose a hybrid model with an initial setup and ongoing support, updates and analytics via subscription.
Is your platform compatible with our current ERP or asset systems?
Yes. JDS solutions are modular and designed to integrate seamlessly with ERP (SAP, Oracle), DCS or cloud storage systems through APIs.
What support do you provide after deployment?
Full onboarding, documentation, training, post-deployment support (SLA), updates and a dedicated customer success team.
