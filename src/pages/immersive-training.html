FAQs

Immersive training questions

Can the VR/CBT modules be customised for our plant or equipment?
Yes. We develop bespoke training simulations based on your SOPs, 3D environments and plant layout.
Do you provide hardware for VR training?
We can supply, recommend or integrate with your existing devices (Meta Quest, HTC Vive, etc.).
How do you measure learning outcomes or safety awareness?
Our system tracks user performance, scores, error rates and completion, with analytics dashboards for HR or EHS leads.
How long does deployment take?
VR/CBT modules can typically be deployed in 2–4 weeks, depending on content complexity.
