Transforming complex industries with XR, Smart Labelling & AI Analytics

We help asset-heavy industries train better, operate smarter and stay safer — tackling industry-specific challenges through measurable transformation.

Energy & Power Plants

Key challenges

  1. High-risk environments with strict safety mandates
  2. Unplanned outages due to reactive maintenance
  3. Paper-based documentation and inspection delays
  4. Traditional training lacks realism for emergencies
  5. Difficulty tracking safety violations
  6. Limited analytics for crew performance

JDS solutions

  • VR-based hazard simulations & safety walkthroughs
  • Smart labelling for turbines, panels and switchgear
  • Digital twins for equipment diagnostics and SOP overlays
  • AiVA for PPE detection, zone monitoring & fire alerts
  • CBT modules for routine safety refreshers
  • Real-time dashboards for maintenance and inspection logs

FAQs

Can JDS solutions integrate with our existing SCADA and ERP systems?
Yes. Our platforms are modular and designed to integrate with most industrial control and enterprise systems.
How do you ensure safety data captured in the field is audit-ready?
All data is logged, timestamped and traceable with blockchain-secured trails for audit compliance.
Do the VR modules simulate actual plant environments and equipment?
Absolutely. We create plant-specific virtual replicas and simulations tailored to your facility layout and processes.

Key challenges

  1. Remote, hazardous, explosion-prone zones
  2. Complex paper-based inspections & SOPs
  3. Training for emergency response is high risk
  4. Limited visibility into field crew compliance
  5. Difficulty tracking corrosion or structural degradation
  6. Logistical complexity of offshore asset audits

JDS solutions

  • AR-guided SOPs & inspections using DDAM
  • 3D digital twins of vessels & field equipment
  • VR-based emergency evacuation & fire response drills
  • AiVA for leak, flame, PPE & fatigue detection

Our system uses thermal cameras and AI-based visual detection to instantly alert and notify predefined contacts.

We offer immersive VR-based safety and emergency response drills aligned with offshore regulatory standards.
