The problem

Frontline workers need the right information, right now

In high-risk, asset-heavy industries, most facilities still rely on paper-based manuals or outdated systems. DDAM is designed for industrial B2B environments like power plants, oil & gas, maritime and logistics.

Once scanned, each label gives real-time access to:

  • O&M manuals
  • Engineering drawings (P&IDs, layouts)
  • Historical maintenance records
  • Compliance certificates & safety info

FAQs

DDAM questions

What is smart labelling, and how does it work?
Smart labelling uses QR codes, OCR, metal tags or GPS identifiers to digitise every asset for instant data access via phone, tablet or smart glasses.
Can we store our data on our servers instead of yours?
Yes. We support hybrid cloud setups — sensitive data can remain on your on-prem server, and general data on ours.
Does it work offline in remote or offshore locations?
Yes. Offline syncing is supported for field conditions like offshore rigs or warehouses with weak connectivity.
