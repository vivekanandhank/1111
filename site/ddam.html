01The challenge

Frontline workers need the right information at the right time

In high-risk, asset-heavy industries, most facilities still rely on paper-based manuals or outdated systems.

DDAM is designed for industrial B2B environments like power plants, oil & gas, maritime and logistics. Once scanned, each label provides real-time access to:

  • O&M manuals
  • Engineering drawings (P&IDs, layouts)
  • Historical maintenance records
  • Compliance certificates & safety info

03Digital twin & platform

Bring field assets to life with spatially mapped replicas

See real-time data overlays, visualise internal structures in AR and follow step-by-step procedures on mobile, tablet or smart glasses — integrated with your IoT or ERP system.

07FAQs

Common questions

Can’t find an answer? Ask our team.

What is smart labelling, and how does it work?
Smart labelling uses QR codes, OCR, metal tags or GPS identifiers to digitise every asset for instant data access via phone, tablet or smart glasses.
Can we store our data on our servers instead of yours?
Yes. We support hybrid cloud setups — sensitive data can remain on your on-prem server, and general data on ours.
Does it work offline in remote or offshore locations?
Yes. Offline syncing is supported for field conditions like offshore rigs or warehouses with weak connectivity.
